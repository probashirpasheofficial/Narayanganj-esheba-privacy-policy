1. Overview & Non-Government Disclaimer

This Privacy Policy explains how Narayanganj Esheba (নারায়ণগঞ্জ ই-সেবা) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our Android mobile application. We are committed to minimizing data collection and protecting your personal privacy.

Independent Civic Directory Notice: Narayanganj Esheba is an independent community information application. It is not an official government app and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the Government of Bangladesh, the Narayanganj District Administration, Bangladesh Police, Bangladesh Fire Service, or Bangladesh Railway. Emergency numbers, office contacts, train timetables, and heritage descriptions are compiled from publicly available sources for citizen convenience.

2. Information We Collect & How We Use It

A. Offline Directory & Local Preferences (Stored On Your Device)

Most features of Narayanganj Esheba work completely offline without signing in. Your bookmarks/favorites, cached service directory entries, and app preferences (selected theme mode, call-confirmation toggle, English subtitle preference, and default upazila filter) are stored locally on your device using Android Room SQLite and Jetpack DataStore. This local preference data is never uploaded to our servers.

B. Optional Google Sign-In (Authentication)

You may optionally sign in using Google Sign-In (via Android Jetpack Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication) if you wish to submit new community listings or sync updates. When you sign in, we receive:

  • Your Firebase User ID (UID)
  • Your Google account display name and email address
  • Email verification status (used for submission security and administrator verification)

C. Community Directory Submissions

If a signed-in user voluntarily submits a new service contact, technician/mistri listing, train schedule suggestion, or tourist place proposal, we store the submitted listing details alongside submitter verification fields (name, email address, phone number, address, and user ID) in Google Cloud Firestore for moderation review.

Strict PII Isolation: Personal submitter information (your phone number, email address, residential address, and account UID attached to a pending submission) is kept private under strict Firestore security rules. It is visible only to you and the verified directory administrator during review and is never exposed to other app users. Only approved directory records are published.

D. Images & Network Requests

Tourist attraction cover photos are loaded over secure HTTPS connections (primarily from Wikimedia Commons) when internet access is available, with locally bundled fallback artwork displayed when offline.

3. What We Do Not Collect

  • No Third-Party Ads or Ad Tracking: The app contains no advertising networks and does not collect Android Advertising IDs.
  • No Background Location Tracking: We do not collect, access, or track your GPS or precise device location.
  • No Silent Calling or Messaging: Tapping Call, Map, or Share only opens your device's standard Dialer, Maps, or Share sheet for your explicit confirmation.
  • No Data Selling: We never sell, rent, or trade user data to any third party.

4. App Permissions Explained

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used only to sync approved directory updates, perform optional Google Sign-In, submit user proposals, and load tourist place photos over HTTPS.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Optional, Android 13+): Requested only if needed to display local administrative moderation alerts or update notices. You can grant or revoke this permission anytime in your Android system settings.

5. Third-Party Services

The application relies on the following trusted infrastructure providers, each governed by its own privacy standards:

  • Google Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore: Used for optional Google Sign-In and cloud moderation/synchronization of community directory listings. Data is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Google Play Services / Credential Manager: Used to securely handle Google Sign-In flows on your Android device.
  • Wikimedia Commons: Used to retrieve public-domain/open-license photographs for Narayanganj tourist landmarks over HTTPS.

6. Data Retention & Account / Data Deletion

We retain authenticated submission records only as long as necessary to verify, moderate, and maintain directory integrity.

  • Delete Local Data: You can clear all local favorites, preferences, and cached directory data anytime by clearing app storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.
  • Request Cloud Data Deletion: You may request deletion of your pending submissions or authentication-linked records by emailing probashirpasheofficial@gmail.com from the Google email address associated with your sign-in. Verified deletion requests are processed within 7 business days.

7. Children's Privacy

Narayanganj Esheba is a general-audience civic utility directory and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted personal details through the app, please contact us so we can promptly remove the data.

8. Changes to This Policy & Contact Us

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app improvements or legal requirements. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
